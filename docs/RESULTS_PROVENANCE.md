## Deliberate exclusions

Other campaigns are excluded: `results/main`, friction/reduced-friction studies, PEFT pilot/follow-up campaigns, extended/mixed-layout flux follow-ups, scratch and source-initialization pilots, the earlier reviewer campaign, and all-station source controls. Within the retained reviewer campaign, `initial_state`, `detached64`, `horizon256` and their unrelated references are excluded. Within source controls, `detached1` and its comparison table are excluded. Mixed-case aggregate tables and the combined reviewer manifest are not copied verbatim because they contain unreported cases; filtered text tables and per-fit configuration/split records preserve the retained evidence.

Resumable optimizer checkpoints, duplicate final weights, unreferenced source-retention outputs, redundant binary per-step error reports, unreferenced per-step error TSVs, animation caches, raw profiling stacks and intermediate run indices are excluded. Interval metrics, original selected weights, rank evidence and the full error histories actually used by the active observed/interior figure remain. Historical reports therefore support figure/table reproduction, but do not constitute a resumable training archive. Fresh runs generate their own complete outputs.

The active results chapter references `parameter_accuracy_flux`, `observed_unobserved_rmse`, `temporal_tradeoff_flux`, `forecast_length_computation_cost_0to12h_flux`, `observation_geometry_flux` and `noise_sensitivity_20cm`. Text-table filtering preserves numeric cells and original row order. Historical absolute paths in text run indices are rewritten to repository-relative paths. Binary records and numeric model/input files are copied unchanged. Fresh runs fingerprint the relocated code as a new campaign and retain historical result provenance separately.
